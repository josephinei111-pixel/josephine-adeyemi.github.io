Case Study 01

Improving Savings Engagement

Business challenge

Customers were not using debit-card savings as frequently as expected.

Customer insight approach

Instead of assuming the reason, I would ask a short, direct question and group responses into useful themes.

“What is the main reason you don't save with your debit card as much as before?”

Action areas

  • Identify whether the issue is awareness, convenience, trust, timing or product experience.
  • Use the findings to improve savings reminders and customer education.
  • Test clearer, shorter messages at appropriate times.
  • Measure whether customer saving activity improves after the changes.
Case Study 02

Customer Complaint → Resolution Workflow

A consistent support workflow helps customers know what is happening while helping support teams avoid missed steps.

01 • RECEIVEUnderstand and acknowledge the issue.
02 • CHECKReview the relevant customer or transaction details.
03 • RESOLVEGive the correct solution or escalate appropriately.
04 • CLOSEConfirm the customer understands the outcome.

Quality measures

First response time • resolution time • repeat contacts • escalation rate • customer satisfaction.

Customer Education

Sample Help-Centre Content

Early Withdrawal / Penalty Fee

Explain when an early withdrawal fee applies, why it applies, and when a customer can make a free withdrawal.

Duplicate Deposit

Explain what happens when a customer makes a duplicate payment and what information support needs to verify it.

Saving With a Debit Card

Give customers a simple explanation of how card-based saving works and how to use it consistently.

My Customer Support Principles

“A good customer response should not only answer the question. It should reduce confusion and make the customer's next step clear.” — Josephine Adeyemi
  • Use simple language.
  • Never make the customer feel blamed for asking for help.
  • Be transparent about what can and cannot be done.
  • Give clear next steps and realistic expectations.
  • Follow through until the issue is properly closed.
